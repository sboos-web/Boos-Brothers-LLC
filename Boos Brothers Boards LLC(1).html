Made at the family workbench

Every Boos Brothers board starts as a rough cut hardwood plank and ends as a one-of-a-kind handcrafted cutting board. We choose each piece for its grain, sand it through five grits, and finish it with food-safe mineral oil and beeswax. No two boards are the same, and each one carries a little bit of our family owned shop with it as it’s shipped out the door.
