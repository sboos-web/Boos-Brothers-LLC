Made at the family workbench

Every Boos Brothers board starts as a rough hardwood plank and ends in the hands of someone we know. We choose each piece for its grain, sand it through five grits, and finish it with food-safe mineral oil and beeswax. No two boards are the same, and each one carries a little of our shop.
